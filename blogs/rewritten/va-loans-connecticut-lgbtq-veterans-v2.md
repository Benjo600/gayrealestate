## LGBTQ Veterans and the VA Loan: The History You Should Know

For much of its history, the VA loan was functionally unavailable to LGBTQ veterans — both because of Don't Ask, Don't Tell and because VA spousal benefits didn't recognize same-sex relationships. That era is over.

**Key milestones:**
- **2010:** Don't Ask, Don't Tell repealed. LGBTQ veterans who serve openly are now equally eligible for all VA benefits.
- **2013:** *United States v. Windsor* began extending federal marriage recognition to same-sex couples, affecting VA spousal and survivor benefits.
- **2015:** *Obergefell v. Hodges* established nationwide marriage equality, fully extending all VA benefits to same-sex spouses on equal terms.
- **Today:** VA loans are available to LGBTQ veterans, same-sex spouses, and surviving same-sex spouses with no restrictions.

If you received a less-than-honorable discharge due to sexual orientation or gender identity under DADT-era policies, your discharge may be upgradeable — and your VA loan eligibility may be restorable. The Modern Military Association of America (MMAA) and OutServe-SLDN both assist with discharge upgrade cases.

## Do You Qualify for a VA Loan?

**Qualifying service (any of the following):**
- 90 consecutive days of active duty during wartime
- 181 days of active duty during peacetime
- 6 years of service in the National Guard or Reserves
- Surviving spouse of a service member who died in the line of duty or from a service-connected disability

**How to confirm:** Request your Certificate of Eligibility (COE) at va.gov, through your lender, or through a VA-approved housing counselor. Most VA-approved lenders can pull your COE directly during pre-approval — you don't need to obtain it yourself first.

## VA Loan Limits in Connecticut (2026)

For veterans with full entitlement, there is no VA-set loan limit — you can borrow any amount your income and credit support with zero down payment. County conforming limits only apply to veterans with reduced entitlement (typically those with an active prior VA loan).

| County | 2026 Conforming Loan Limit |
|---|---|
| Fairfield County (high-cost) | $1,209,750 |
| Hartford, New Haven, Middlesex, Tolland, Windham, New London, Litchfield | $806,500 |

The Fairfield County limit is critical for buyers in Stamford, Norwalk, Greenwich, and Westport — where median prices routinely exceed $700,000.

## The VA Funding Fee: Rates and Exemptions

The VA charges a one-time funding fee at closing, financed into the loan (not paid out of pocket). The fee funds the VA loan program.

| Down Payment | First-Time Use | Subsequent Use |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 5% | 2.15% | 3.30% |
| 5%–9.99% | 1.50% | 1.50% |
| 10%+ | 1.25% | 1.25% |

**Exempt from the funding fee:**
- Veterans receiving VA disability compensation
- Veterans rated eligible for compensation but receiving retirement pay
- Surviving spouses of veterans who died in service or from service-connected disabilities

If you carry any service-connected disability rating, confirm your exemption status with your lender before closing. Exemption can save thousands.

## Choosing a VA-Approved Lender in Connecticut: What LGBTQ Veterans Should Ask

Not all VA-approved lenders are equal — and not all lenders understand how to handle LGBTQ-specific situations (same-sex co-borrower applications, domestic partner income documentation, trans veteran ID situations). Before you commit to a lender, ask:

1. **How many VA loans have you closed in the past 12 months in Connecticut?** A lender who closes 2–3 VA loans a year will be slower and less experienced than one who does dozens.

2. **Are you familiar with CHFA's Government Insured Mortgage program?** If they aren't, you may be leaving a rate advantage on the table.

3. **How do you handle same-sex co-borrower applications?** The answer should be: "Exactly the same as any other co-borrower." If you hear hesitation, find a different lender.

4. **What are your lender-set credit requirements for VA loans?** The VA has no minimum; lenders typically require 580–620. Some require 640+. Know upfront.

5. **Can you obtain my Certificate of Eligibility directly?** A good VA lender does this routinely — you shouldn't need to chase it down yourself.

## LGBTQ-Specific Considerations for Veteran Buyers

**Title and co-borrower options.** If you're buying with a same-sex spouse or partner, your real estate attorney will advise on whether to take title as joint tenants (survivorship rights) or tenants in common (flexible ownership shares). This connects directly to estate planning — don't treat it as a formality.

**VA benefits and estate planning.** If your same-sex spouse is your surviving spouse, they may be eligible for VA loan benefits and death benefits. Ensure your VA records reflect your current marital status and beneficiary designations.

**Trans veterans.** The VA has updated its policies to reflect name and gender marker changes. If you're trans, confirm that your Certificate of Eligibility reflects your current legal name to avoid complications at closing. MMAA provides guidance specific to trans veteran VA situations.

**Discharge upgrades.** Veterans who received less than honorable discharges because of sexual orientation or gender identity may be eligible for discharge upgrades — which can restore VA eligibility. Contact MMAA or OutServe-SLDN for case-specific guidance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can gay veterans use VA loans?**
Yes. VA loans are fully available to LGBTQ veterans with qualifying service on equal terms. Sexual orientation and gender identity are not factors in VA loan eligibility.

**Can same-sex spouses use VA loan benefits?**
Yes. Since *Obergefell v. Hodges* (2015), same-sex marriages are recognized by the federal government including the VA. Same-sex spouses have the same eligibility as opposite-sex spouses for VA loan benefits and survivor benefits.

**Do I need a down payment for a VA loan in Connecticut?**
No — if you have full VA loan entitlement. You can finance 100% of the purchase price with zero down payment.

**What is the VA loan limit in Connecticut in 2026?**
For veterans with full entitlement, there is no VA-set limit. County conforming limits apply to veterans with reduced entitlement: $806,500 in most CT counties, $1,209,750 in Fairfield County.

**What is the VA funding fee?**
A one-time fee of 2.15% (first use, 0% down) that funds the VA loan program and is financed into the loan. Veterans receiving VA disability compensation are exempt.

**Can I stack VA loan benefits with CHFA programs?**
Yes. CHFA's Government Insured Mortgage program includes VA loans at a below-market rate (6.125% as of June 2026). This combination can be one of the most cost-effective financing options available to Connecticut first-time buyers.

**I received a less-than-honorable discharge. Can I still get a VA loan?**
Potentially yes, if you pursue a discharge upgrade. Veterans discharged for sexual orientation or gender identity under DADT-era policies are often eligible for upgrades that restore VA benefits. MMAA and OutServe-SLDN handle these cases.
