Here is a comprehensive overview of the current CHFA (Connecticut Housing Finance Authority) mortgage interest rates and programs as of **June 3–6, 2026**, sourced directly from [chfa.org](https://www.chfa.org/homebuyer-mortgage-interest-rates/).

***

## HFA Preferred™ Loan Program

This is CHFA's flagship conventional loan for first-time homebuyers. Key details:[^2]

- 30-year fixed-rate mortgage at **6.500%** (APR 6.600%–7.000%)[^1]
- Requires **no upfront mortgage insurance** and offers low monthly MI costs[^3]
- Borrowers must be first-time buyers (or not have owned a home in 3 years) unless purchasing in a Targeted Area[^2]
- Income must not exceed CHFA's county-level income limits; purchase price must not exceed CHFA Sales Price Limits[^2]
- Minimum **\$3,000 down payment assistance** available in conjunction with a DAP loan[^2]

***

## Time To Own – Forgivable Down Payment Assistance

This is CHFA's most generous assistance program, currently active with fresh funding:[^4]

- **0% interest rate, no monthly payments required**[^5]
- **10% of the principal forgiven annually** — fully forgiven at 10-year anniversary[^1]
- Can cover **up to 20% down payment + up to 5% closing costs**[^5]
- Loan amounts: **up to \$25,000** for standard areas; **up to \$50,000** for properties in "High" or "Very High Opportunity Areas" per the CT Opportunity Map[^6]
- As of **June 1, 2026**, \$33,231,490 remains available for reservations, after the CT State Bond Commission authorized an additional \$10,000,000 on May 29, 2026[^4]
- Minimum loan amount is **\$3,000**[^4]
- Must be a current CT resident for at least 3 years and must pair the loan with a CHFA first mortgage[^4]

***

## Income Limits \& Purchase Price Limits

CHFA does not publish universal statewide caps — limits are **set by county and household size**, tied to Area Median Income (AMI). Key rules:[^2]

- For **Time To Own**, borrowers at **≤80% AMI** get up to 100% of eligible assistance; borrowers between **80%–100% AMI** get up to 75%[^6]
- The DAP loan is capped at **\$15,000** for standard homebuyers[^3]
- For current county-by-county income and sales price limit tables, CHFA publishes an updated PDF at [chfa.org/sales-income-limits](https://www.chfa.org)[^2]
