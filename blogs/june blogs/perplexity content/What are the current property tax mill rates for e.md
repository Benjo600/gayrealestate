# What are the current property tax mill rates for every town in Connecticut as of 2025 or 2026? Provide a complete list sorted from lowest to highest mill rate. Include the grand list year the mill rate applies to. Source from the Connecticut Office of Policy and Management (OPM) or official town assessor data.

Here is the comprehensive list of Connecticut property tax mill rates for **FY 2025–2026**, based on the **October 1, 2024 Grand List**. These rates apply to real property and are sourced from the Connecticut Office of Policy and Management (OPM) and official town data.[^1][^2][^3]

***

## Key Facts

- The mill rates for FY 2025–2026 are based on the **October 1, 2024 Grand List**[^1]
- **Lowest rate:** Washington at **10.85 mills**[^2]
- **Highest rate:** Hartford at **68.95 mills**[^2]
- **State average:** 28.22 mills across all 169 towns[^3]
- Motor vehicles are capped statewide at **32.46 mills**[^2]
- Properties are assessed at **70% of fair market value**[^2]

## Special Taxing Districts

Several towns have **additional fire district or special district mills** layered on top of the base rate:[^4][^2]

- **Torrington – Lakeridge Tax District:** 61.32 mills (dropped ~55% from prior year)
- **Norwich:** City Consolidation District total = 40.47 mills (base 34.51 + 5.96 CCD)
- **Middletown:** Combined rate up to 38.90 mills (base 31.70 + fire district)
- **West Haven:** Fire district surcharges range 7.29–8.95 additional mills
- **Bloomfield:** Center FD and Blue Hills FD add 1–2.5 mills above base

For the **most precise, parcel-specific mill rate**, always verify directly with the town assessor or the official [CT OPM Mill Rates page](https://portal.ct.gov/opm/igpp/publications/mill-rates), which publishes the authoritative annual data.
