## Notes on data quality

A few towns did not return a clean town-level median sale price from the available sources in this pass, especially Mystic, Westport, and Litchfield. For Glastonbury, Zillow returned average home value and days-to-pending rather than a clear median sale price, so I listed the value metric separately.[^8][^18]

## Source conventions

Redfin usually reports “median sale price” and average days on market over the last 3 months, while Zillow often reports “home value,” “median sale price,” and “days to pending.” Realtor.com can provide median listing price and days on market, which is useful when sale-price data is sparse.[^2][^3][^4][^10][^11][^15][^1]
