# What We Do

The LGBTQ+ Real Estate Alliance is the leading advocate for the LGBTQ+ community in real estate as we work towards eliminating housing discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity. Our 3,500-plus LGBTQ+ and ally members benefit from our real estate industry voice, professional development and referral opportunities along with national events and local connectivity through our growing list of 30+ chapters nationwide. Founded in 2020, the LGBTQ+ Real Estate Alliance has a mission to advocate, elevate and celebrate!

[Learn More](https://realestatealliance.org/about-us/)
