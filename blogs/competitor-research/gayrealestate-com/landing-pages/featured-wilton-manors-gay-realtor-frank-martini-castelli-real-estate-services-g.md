Frank moved to Fort Lauderdale in the summer of 2010. He brought with him a wealth of real estate experience and knowledge from San Francisco, California where he had been helping clients since 2006 as both a salesman and a broker associate. Upon moving to Fort Lauderdale, Frank hunted for just the right real estate company to call home and found the professionalism and charm of John Castelli and the entire office at Castelli Real Estate Services irresistible. Graduating college with a B.S. in both Math and Physics, Frank then embarked on a long career in software design, development and consulting. After having remodeled many of his own properties he choose to devote his time to helping others find their dream homes and real estate investments. His easy going, confident nature makes him a joy to work with while his tenacity and work ethic means he produces results. Frank was born and raised in New York City, attended college in Buffalo, NY and graduate school in Denver Colorado where he purchased his first home in 1986. He then moved to San Francisco in 1989 and called it home for over 20 years. In Fort Lauderdale, he enjoys the unique building characteristics, year round warm weather, beaches and botanicals. In his free time, Frank enjoys working out, beaches, scuba diving, the arts, scifi and the guilty pleasure of a video game or two.
