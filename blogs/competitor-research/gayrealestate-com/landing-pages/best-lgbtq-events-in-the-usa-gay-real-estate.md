The USA is renowned for its fantastic Pride events worldwide, and rightly so! Home to some of the largest celebrations in the world, the States brings together the LGBTQ+ community and allies for parades, parties, and festivals.

These events come in all shapes and sizes, so if you’re trying to decide which to attend, you’ve come to the right place. We’ve listed the best LGBTQ+ events across several states, so you can plan your itinerary and make the most of the festivities.

## The Biggest Pride Events in the USA

Pride events in the US bring millions of visitors each year, combining pride with protest as participants march in support of the LGBTQ+ community. Complete with flamboyant floats, passionate performers, and striking community spirit, these Pride events are a must for those who like a crowd.

### NYC Pride

NYC Pride is one of the biggest Pride events not only in the USA, but in the world. Held in June to commemorate the Stonewall Riots of 1969, New York City Pride attracts over a million people and features a huge march from [Midtown to lower Manhattan](https://www.gayrealestate.com/blog/2025/11/best-gay-neighborhoods-in-nyc), honoring the historic Stonewall Inn.

A number of New York Pride events take place during Pride Week, including YouthPride, PrideFest, Pride in Times Square, and plenty of Pride parties. You must be registered as part of an official organization to march, but New York’s main Pride events are free to attend.

### LA Pride

Held in the second week of June, LA Pride features the famous LA Pride Parade, which consistently attracts over 100,000 people to Hollywood Boulevard. Full of bands, floats, dancers, and celebrities, LA Pride is a show-stopping event guaranteed to bring the energy.

Accompanying the parade is Pride Village, a free and vibrant street-festival offering over 100 vendors, multiple bars, exhibitors, and even live-on-stage performances. Considered as one of the best gay events in Los Angeles, it’s also the perfect place to grab a drink and a bite to eat.

### Chicago Pride

Two-day festival, anyone? Chicago Pride Fest is a huge event split into three stages: music, food, and the Proud Pet Parade, where pets are dressed in colorful costumes and shown off during the march! Over 100,000 attend each year to enjoy the Pride celebrations.

As one of the USA’s biggest LGBTQ+ events, the Chicago Pride Parade draws in over one million people and features a variety of performers, marching bands, and over 100 floats. Paraders start in Uptown, pass through [Northalsted](https://www.gayrealestate.com/blog/2025/11/gay-neighborhoods-in-chicago-for-lgbtq-homebuyers), and conclude in Lincoln Park, bringing a sea of rainbow with them. It’s not hard to see why Chicago is among the [best gay cities in the USA](https://www.gayrealestate.com/blog/2026/01/best-gay-cities-in-the-usa-where-to-buy-live).

### Seattle Pride

Seattle Pride is home to several fabulous and free LGBTQ+ events, including Seattle Pride in the Park: a festival complete with live music, performances, and food trucks. Another major Pride event is the Seattle Pride Parade, known as the largest Pride parade in Washington. Over 300,000 attend every year to march down 4th Avenue.

The celebrations continue with PrideFest at the Seattle Center, featuring multiple stages, live performers, and a wide range of food vendors. You can also attend the Queer/Pride Festival, a three-day event on Capitol Hill; Trans Pride, a march and rally organised by the Gender Justice League; and Wildrose Pride, a lively three-day Pride party hosted by the iconic lesbian bar.

### San Francisco Pride

Attracting over one million visitors each year, San Francisco Pride has to make the list of the USA’s biggest LGBTQ+ events. The San Francisco Pride Parade and the Pride Celebration both take place over Pride Weekend (the last weekend of June) and are free to attend.

Trans March, one of the world’s largest Trans Pride events, also takes place in [San Francisco](https://www.gayrealestate.com/blog/2026/03/why-san-francisco-is-the-gay-capital-of-the-usa), with participants marching through Dolores Park and the Mission District. The Dyke March, centered on lesbian visibility and protest, follows a similar route on a different day. Those who love a street party can attend Pink Saturday in the Castro neighborhood where you can dance the night away.

### Washington DC Pride

Capital Pride takes place in June, with visitors flocking to join Washington DC’s multiple Pride celebrations. Full of floats, performers, music, and crowds, the iconic Capital Pride Parade march passes through Logan Circle and 14th Street, showcasing queer joy and resilience.

The Capital Pride Festival is also hugely popular, featuring performers, multiple stages, and hundreds of vendors. You can also attend the Capital Pride Concert and catch talented musical and drag artists on-stage. DC Black Pride, widely considered the first Black Pride event in the USA, offers everything from seminars and workshops to parties and performances.

### Denver Pride

Encompassing multiple LGBTQ+ events from queer hiking to music festivals, Denver Pride honors queer culture through several mediums. One of its three major Pride events includes Denver PrideFest, a free two-day event consisting of the Vizzy Denver Pride Parade, marches, and live performances.

The Pride Parade is one of Colorado’s biggest LGBTQ+ events, bringing hundreds of thousands to downtown Denver each June. If you fancy getting active, Denver’s Pride 5k invites participants to run, walk, or sashay through the city to support LGBTQ+ non-profits. This is a ticketed event, so be sure to register sooner rather than later.

### Atlanta Black Pride

As one of the largest Black LGBTQ+ celebrations in the world, Atlanta Black Pride is held each Labor Day weekend and attracts visitors from all over the country. The weekend focuses on community, culture, and visibility, featuring key events like the vibrant parade, the Pure Heat Community Festival, and plenty of Pride parties.

Thousands attend the free and famous Community Festival, drawn in by live performances, local vendors, community resources, and competitions. Centered in the 200-acre Piedmont Park, participants celebrate in the open-air surrounded by music and live entertainment.

## Smaller Yet Iconic Pride Events

Not all Pride events have to be teeming with people to be unforgettable. For those wishing to avoid major cities and opt instead for more intimate, charismatic spaces, we’ve listed some incredible Pride events that take place on a smaller scale.

### Provincetown Pride

Combining Pride celebrations with coastal beauty, Provincetown Pride is well-known for its LGBTQ+ background and welcomes thousands to its charming streets over Pride Month. Visitors attend the Pride rally and the Sashay to Tea Parade, which concludes with the iconic Tea Dance. (For those who don’t know, this involves mingling, music, and drinks!)

If you fancy exploring the work of LGBTQ+ artists and artisans, you can attend multiple art shows and the Pride Market, where sellers and local organizations come together to share their work. All of Provincetown’s events are infused with its creative energy, making it a truly unique Pride experience.

### Asheville Pride

Tucked in the Blue Ridge Mountains, Asheville Pride reflects the city’s vibrancy and warmth. Each September, Pack Square Park is filled with live music, drag performances, and a colorful downtown parade as part of the Asheville Pride Festival. Small businesses, artists, locals, and visitors alike champion queer voices during the festivities.

Asheville is also well-known for its drag shows, including The Blue Ridge Pride Drag Pageant and Asheville Drag Brunches, which showcase fantastic performances from both contestants and famous guests. These events take place in late summer, so be sure to plan your trip and book in advance.

### Salt Lake City Pride

Pride celebrations in Salt Lake City are centered around the Utah Pride Festival, which takes place in early June. Their festivities are separate, though, with SLC Pride consisting of smaller, more intimate LGBTQ+ events. To kick off Pride Weekend, you can attend chilled rooftop gatherings and queer mixers at cafes, followed by local drag performances and variety shows.

SLC Pride’s focus is on community, offering queer art shows, craft markets, park picnics, themed nightlife nights, as well as alcohol-free events and mindfulness activities. What’s more, if you prefer the energy of crowds, you have the best of both worlds.

### LGBTQ+ Events: Arts and Culture

LGBTQ+ celebrations don’t always take the form of Pride events, and the USA is home to various festivals that celebrate cultural expression and local artistry. Whether you’re a music lover, film fanatic, or simply want to try all there is to do, dive into our list of LGBTQ+ events below.

### Northalsted Market Days, Chicago

As one of the USA’s largest LGBTQ+ street festivals, Northalsted Market Days spans over the Northalsted neighborhood with multiple stages, live music, drag shows, food vendors, and local artists. With so much to do and explore, it’s no wonder it attracts over a hundred thousand attendees.

Part of the charm comes from the walkable streets and the ease of dipping in and out of the attractions; from browsing stalls to wandering between music sets, there’s always something to do. The celebrations last for three days in early August, so you can enjoy these LGBTQ+ events to the fullest.

### Southern Decadence, New Orleans

Southern Decadence takes place over Labor Day weekend and is renowned for its bold, celebratory energy. What began as a small gathering has now become a major LGBTQ+ event featuring parades, themed parties, and various performances across the neighborhood.

The main parade typically begins on the Sunday and weaves through the historic French Quarter. Bourbon Street Parties are always abuzz, full of street DJs, big crowds, and a lot of dancing. There is also no shortage of bar events, pool parties, or drag shows, making Southern Decadence a hub of entertainment.

### Frameline Film Festival, San Francisco

Frameline is the longest-running and one of the most respected LGBTQ+ film festivals. Often held in late June, it showcases a wide range of films from indie stories to international features. These are screened in cinemas across the city, with tickets available per-showing or as multi-film passes.

If you want a chilled night out as a tourist or [local](https://www.gayrealestate.com/blog/2025/09/a-locals-guide-to-lgbtq-life-in-san-francisco) in one of the [gayest cities in the USA](https://www.gayrealestate.com/blog/2025/11/top-16-gayest-cities-in-the-us-for-lgbtq-relocation), this queer film festival is the place to be. Offering a relaxed and inclusive atmosphere, it’s also the perfect place to connect with fellow fans, visual artists, and LGBTQ+ culture through film.

### Outfest, Los Angeles

Storytelling and representation are the focus of Outfest, a queer film festival that delivers powerful LGBTQ+ cinema. With films scheduled all year-round, you don’t have to wait until Pride Month to get in on the action. Queer voices and creativity are championed with Outfest offering support and mentorship to queer filmmakers.

To attend, you’ll need tickets or passes, and you have the option of watching in-person in Los Angeles or online through the Outfest Screening Room. Whichever you choose, you’ll be joining like-minded film fanatics in supporting LGBTQ+ talent.

### Bushwig, New York

As one of the biggest drag festivals on the East Coast, Bushwig celebrates drag, performance art, and queer creativity in all its forms. Held in early September, it’s a two-day festival filled with drag performances, DJ sets, live music, and dancing on multiple stages.

Bushwig is also a great opportunity to grab some food at the numerous food stalls, browse pop-up shops, and attend after-parties. By highlighting emerging talent and established drag artists, this LGBTQ+ event creates a space where everyone feels seen.

## FAQs for The Best LGBTQ+ Events in the USA

After reading all about some of the biggest and best LGBTQ+ events in the USA, you may have more queries than before! Here are the most frequently asked questions surrounding Pride and LGBTQ+ events.

### What is Pride Month all about?

Pride Month is celebrated in June to commemorate the Stonewall Riots, a historic turning point for LGBTQ+ rights. Now, June is a celebration of LGBTQ+ culture, identity, and visibility, while protesting against discrimination and advocating for equality. This is why many large Pride events take place in June, although others run throughout the year.

### When is Pride Week?

In the USA, Pride Week is most commonly celebrated during the last week of June, leading up to Pride Weekend.

### What’s the biggest Pride Parade in the USA?

The biggest Pride Parade in the USA is NYC Pride March, which regularly attracts over a million spectators and tens of thousands of participants.
